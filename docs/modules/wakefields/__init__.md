# `slipstream.wakefields`

[Source](../../../src/slipstream/wakefields/__init__.py) · [Documentation index](../../README.md)

## Physics

Exports longitudinal impedance and short/long-range wake models. Wake tables/phasors encode their own physical unit and sign conventions.

## Usage

Import `Impedance`, `ShortRangeWake`, `LongRangeWake`, `Resonator` and conversion/phasor helpers; transverse and chamber solvers live in their submodules.
