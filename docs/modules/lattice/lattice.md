# `slipstream.lattice.lattice`

[Source](../../../src/slipstream/lattice/lattice.py) · [Documentation index](../../README.md)

## Physics

Stores longitudinal ring parameters and derives relativistic speed, revolution period, harmonic number, synchronous phase, synchrotron frequency and natural bunch length. Energy/rest mass are eV; RF voltage is volts.

### Limits and conventions

- Use the synchronous-phase model only for a physically supported RF equilibrium; a linac does not require this ring object.

## Usage

Construct `Lattice` from circumference, reference total energy, momentum compaction, RF, energy loss and equilibrium damping/spread, or load `from_yaml`. `to_yaml` saves scalar parameters.
